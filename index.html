Baby Names for Couples

Find baby names you both love

A swipe-to-match app that helps couples agree on a baby name together. Free to download, no account needed.

♥

Baby Names for Couples is a free iOS app that helps partners choose a baby name together. Each partner swipes through names independently: right to love, left to pass. When both partners like the same name, it appears on a shared matches list. The app is free to download and use, with no account, no subscription, and no ads.

The mechanic is similar to popular dating apps: swipe through cards, see what you match on, and build a shortlist from there. Instead of people, the cards are baby names. Instead of a date, the outcome is a name you both agree on.

♡

How it works

1

Download and start swiping. Get the app from the App Store. No sign-up or email address required. Pick your name preferences (boy, girl, or both) and choose from collections like Rare Gems, Vintage Revival, Latin & Hispanic, Gender Neutral, Literary & Fictional, and more. Start swiping through names right away.

2

Connect with your partner. One of you shares an invite link from the app. The other taps it and you are connected. No email, no login, no account. Everything syncs between your devices in real time from that point on.

3

Discover your matches. When both of you swipe right on the same name, it lands on your shared matches list. Star your favourites to build a shortlist, reorder them however you like, and work your way down to the one name you both love.

What makes Baby Names for Couples different

Pronunciation audio for every name. Every name in the app includes built-in audio so you can hear exactly how it sounds before you add it to your list. Tap the pronunciation button on any name card to listen. This helps with unfamiliar names, names from other cultures, and names where the spelling does not make the pronunciation obvious.

Popularity trends across 21 countries. See whether a name is rising, falling, or holding steady in countries including the UK, US, Australia, India, Japan, Brazil, and more. Explore trends going back decades to understand how a name has changed over time. Useful for parents who want a name that feels current, or one that stands out.

24 curated collections spanning cultures and styles. Browse names organised by origin, theme, and style. Collections include Rare Gems, Vintage Revival, Gender Neutral, Literary & Fictional, Latin & Hispanic, Nature-Inspired, Celtic & Irish, Classic, Modern, and more.

Submit any name you want. Have a name in mind that is not in the app? Type it in and the app generates its meaning, origin, and pronunciation automatically. The name then appears in your partner's swipe stack as a regular card, without them knowing you submitted it. If they swipe right too, it is a match. This works for family names, cultural names, or anything you want your partner to consider.

No account needed. Baby Names for Couples uses anonymous device-based identity. There is no sign-up form, no email address, and no login screen. To connect with a partner, share an invite link. That is the only setup required.

Free to download and use. Start swiping through baby names for free. There are no ads, no subscription, and no recurring charges.

Works solo or as a couple. Partner pairing is optional. You can use Baby Names for Couples entirely on your own to explore names, hear pronunciations, view popularity trends, and build your own favourites list. Connect with a partner whenever you are ready to start discovering mutual matches.

Frequently asked questions

How does Baby Names for Couples work?

Do both partners need to download the app?
