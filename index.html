Getting started

Baby Names for Couples is free to download and you can start swiping straight away. No account or sign-up is needed. To connect with your partner, one of you creates an invite code and the other enters it. That’s it.

💕

Connecting with your partner

To pair with a partner, go to the Partner tab and tap Create Invite Code. Share the code with your partner, and they can enter it on the same screen. Once connected, any names you both swipe right on will appear as matches on both devices in real time.

If you need to disconnect, you can do so from the Profile screen. Your swipe history and matches are preserved.

✨

Purchases

All purchases in Baby Names for Couples are one-time. There are no subscriptions and no recurring charges. When one partner buys a collection, the other partner gets access too, automatically.

Restoring purchases: If you reinstall the app or switch devices, your purchases can be restored automatically. Go to the Profile screen and tap Restore Purchases.
📜

Deleting your data

You can delete all of your data at any time from within the app. Go to the Profile tab, scroll to the Account section, and tap Delete Account. This permanently removes your display name, swipe history, matches, and connection data from our servers.

You can also email us and we will delete your data within 30 days.

🔧

Something not working?

If you run into a problem, we want to know about it. When you email us, it helps to include:

Your device model (e.g. iPhone 15), iOS version (e.g. iOS 18.4), and a brief description of what happened. A screenshot is always helpful too.
