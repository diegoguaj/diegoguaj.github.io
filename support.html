Getting started

How do I start using the app?

Download Baby Names for Couples from the App Store. Open it and choose whether you are looking for boy names, girl names, or both. Pick a collection and start swiping. No account or sign-up is needed.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app uses anonymous device-based identity. There is no sign-up form, email address, or login screen. You download the app and start immediately.

Is the app free?

Yes. Baby Names for Couples is free to download and use. There are no subscriptions and no ads.

Pairing with a partner

How do I connect with my partner?

Tap the partner icon in the app and share an invite link with your partner by text, email, or any messaging app. Your partner taps the link and you are connected. Everything syncs from that point.

Do both of us need to download the app?

To get name matches, both partners download the app on their own iPhone. One shares an invite link and the other taps it to connect.

Can I use the app without a partner?

Yes. Solo mode gives you full access to swiping, pronunciation audio, popularity trends, and your own favourites list. You can connect with a partner later without losing anything you have saved.

What happens if I disconnect from my partner?

You can disconnect from the Profile screen at any time. Your swipe history, matches, and favourites are all preserved. If you reconnect later, everything carries over.

Swiping and matches

How does matching work?

Each partner swipes through names independently. Swipe right to love a name, left to pass. When both partners swipe right on the same name, it is a match and the name is added to your shared matches list.

Can my partner see which names I swiped on?

No. Individual swipes are private. The only names visible to both partners are mutual matches. Your "I Liked" tab shows names you loved and your partner's status (not yet seen, loved, or passed), but your partner does not see a corresponding view.

Can I suggest a name to my partner?

Yes. Suggest a name and it appears in your partner's next few cards so they see it sooner. They still swipe on it independently.

Can I undo a swipe?

Swipes cannot be undone individually. If you accidentally passed on a name, you may encounter it again if you reset a collection or if your partner suggests it to you.

Data and privacy

What data does the app collect?

Baby Names for Couples collects the minimum data needed to provide the service: an anonymous device identifier, your name preferences, swipe history, and partner connection data. No personal information such as your name, email, or location is collected. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete my data?

Go to the Profile tab, scroll to the Account section, and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your display name, swipe history, matches, and connection data from our servers. This action cannot be undone. You can also email us and we will delete your data within 30 days.
